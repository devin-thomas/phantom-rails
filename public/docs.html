GET /api/v1/postings Search & Filter Canonical Postings

Executes parameterized PostgreSQL full-text search with explainable field weights (Title +8, Company +5, Location +3, Excerpt +1), compound filters, and HMAC-signed keyset pagination cursors.

ParameterTypeDescription
qstring (max 120 chars)Keyword search query. Requires all meaningful English lexemes to match.
companystringCase-insensitive exact normalized company name.
locationstringNormalized city/state location filter.
remote_typeenumonsite, hybrid, remote, or unknown.
employment_typeenumfull_time, part_time, contract, internship, temporary, unknown.
min_salary_usdintegerAnnual compensation minimum in USD. Unknown/hourly wages are excluded.
sortenumrelevance (default if q present), newest, company, title.
limitinteger (1-50)Results per page (default: 20).
cursorstringOpaque HMAC-signed keyset cursor token.
